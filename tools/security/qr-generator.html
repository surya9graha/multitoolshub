Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is a QR code?

A: A QR (Quick Response) code is a matrix barcode that stores alphanumeric data in a two-dimensional grid, readable by camera sensors.

Q: What is QR code error correction?

A: Error correction uses Reed-Solomon mathematical algorithms to reconstruct data. This allows the code to scan even if it is dirty or damaged.

Q: Can I use custom colors for my QR code?

A: Yes. However, ensure there is high contrast between the dark foreground and light background to keep the code scan-ready.

Q: Do QR codes expire?

A: Static QR codes generated by our tool link directly to your content and do not expire. They will work as long as the destination URL remains active.

Q: How much text data can a QR code store?

A: A standard QR code can store up to 4,296 alphanumeric characters or 7,089 numeric digits, depending on the grid size and error correction settings.

Q: Is my QR code data saved on the server?

A: No. All code generation occurs locally within your browser tab, ensuring your data remains private.

Q: Why won't my generated QR code scan?

A: Common causes include low color contrast, scanning in low-light environments, or trying to encode too much data at a low resolution.

Q: Can I generate a QR code offline?

A: Yes, once the page loads, the generation scripts run locally, allowing offline use.
