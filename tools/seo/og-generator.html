Introduction to Social Metadata and Open Graph Protocol

When users share links on social media platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, or Twitter, the platform displays a summary card containing a headline, thumbnail image, and description. Open Graph meta tags tell social platforms how to format this card. Our online Open Graph Generator formats these tags based on your inputs.

Our tool generates Open Graph and Twitter Card tags in standard HTML format, ready to paste into your website's header.

Why Structured Social Meta Tags Matter for SEO

Structured Open Graph tags don't directly impact search rankings, but they do improve how your pages look when shared. Eye-catching social cards can increase click-through rates (CTR) from social media channels.

Real-Life Use Cases for Open Graph Tags

  • Marketing Campaigns: Formatting unique page descriptions for social shares.
  • Blog Customization: Setting custom social card thumbnails for individual blog posts.
  • CMS Theme Optimization: Creating default metadata rules for theme templates.

Understanding the Key Open Graph Properties

An effective social metadata block includes several key tags:

  • og:title: The headline displayed on the social card (recommended: under 60 characters).
  • og:type: The type of content (e.g. website, article, profile).
  • og:image: The URL of the image thumbnail (recommended: 1200x630 pixels).
  • og:url: The canonical link for the page.
  • og:description: A brief description of the page (recommended: under 110 characters).
