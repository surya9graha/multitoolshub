Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do robots.txt generator files help my SEO?

A: A robots.txt file guides search bots on which pages to crawl or avoid. Restricting crawlers from scanning administrative or private directories saves crawl budget and prevents duplicate indexation issues.

Q: What are Open Graph tags, and why are they necessary?

A: Open Graph (OG) tags are special meta tags that control how your website's preview card is displayed on platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn. A descriptive title and high-resolution image tag increase CTR by up to 150%.

Q: Do your analyzers run live requests on remote websites?

A: Certain analyzer tools evaluate local parameters client-side. For analyzers that inspect live URL meta tags, minor requests are funneled through secure cloud APIs purely to retrieve header schemas, without storing queries.
